## Step 5 — Strategy Evaluation

**Score: 2.5/5** — Tesla retains strategic assets and reinvestment capacity, but current capital returns are below the cited cost of capital, execution demands are high, and customer-sentiment evidence is incomplete.

### Return metrics — 1/5

TTM ROE was **4.67%**, ROIC **5.46%**, and ROCE **3.78%**, versus a provider-estimated WACC of **14.25%** ([StockAnalysis statistics](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tsla/statistics/), through 2026-06-30). On this snapshot, ROIC is below WACC. Verified 5-, 10-, and 15-year averages are **data unavailable — not verified**.

### Capital allocation — 3/5

Tesla paid no dividend, and shares outstanding increased **0.58% year over year** to **3.950B** ([StockAnalysis statistics](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tsla/statistics/), accessed 2026-09-12). Capital is being directed toward organic investment: Q2 2026 capex was **$5.789B**, above quarterly operating cash flow ([StockAnalysis quarterly cash flow](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/tsla/financials/cash-flow-statement/?p=quarterly)). FY2025 and Q2 2026 R&D expense are **data unavailable — not verified**.

### Competitive moat — 4/5

Tesla's brand, vertically integrated vehicle/software architecture, manufacturing scale, charging footprint, and accumulated fleet data form mutually reinforcing advantages. The score is constrained because automotive pricing and margin compression indicate those advantages do not currently translate into strong pricing power.

### Customer and brand — 2/5

Current NPS, app-store rating trends, Trustpilot trends, and a reproducible customer-sentiment sample are **data unavailable — not verified**. The brand remains globally prominent, but a score above 2 would require current evidence rather than model knowledge.

The combined score is the average of Return Metrics 1, Capital Allocation 3, Competitive Moat 4, and Customer/Brand 2.

### Final Verdict — Neutral — monitor

The skill's decision matrix maps an average quality score (11–15) with an expensive valuation to **Neutral — monitor**.

**Bull case:** Tesla's net-cash balance sheet, brand, integrated ecosystem, and energy/services growth provide the resources and strategic options to restore growth. Successful scaling of autonomy, energy storage, software, or commercial vehicles could produce operating leverage not reflected in current automotive margins.

**Bear case:** Revenue and earnings weakened in FY2025, current capital returns are below the cited WACC, and Q2 2026 FCF was negative during a capex-heavy period. At 379.22x trailing earnings and 250.49x FCF, the share price embeds substantial execution success while governance and regulatory evidence remains incomplete.
